Privacy

The short version: there are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and your game never leaves your browser.

What's stored, and where

Your game (your play money, positions, history and stats) and your settings (theme, sound, the coin you picked last) are kept in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. They're never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser, or Reset everything in the game's menu, deletes them.

Who your browser talks to

  • This site (hosted on GitHub Pages), to load the game. GitHub may log requests, as any web host does.
  • Coinbase (api.exchange.coinbase.com and ws-feed.exchange.coinbase.com), for live prices and price history. These are public market data feeds: your browser sends no information about you or your game, but Coinbase sees your IP address, as any server you connect to does. See Coinbase's privacy policy.

Nothing else: no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. The site's Content Security Policy blocks everything except these two.
