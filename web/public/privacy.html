Privacy

The short version: there are no accounts, no cookies from this site, no analytics and no ads. Your game is saved only in your own browser, and never leaves it.

What's stored, and where

The game keeps its state in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. It isn't sent to this site, to Coinbase or to anyone else, and nobody (including me) can see it. That's:

  • your game: play money, open positions, the running round, history and stats;
  • the moment it last watched the prices, so it can check what happened to your positions while the page was closed;
  • your settings: the theme, sound on or off, and the coin you picked last.

Everyone's game is their own, in their own browser. Come back in the same browser and your game is still there; open the site on another device, in another browser or in a private window, and you start fresh with $10,000. Reset everything in the game's menu, or clearing this site's data in your browser, deletes it all.

This storage only makes the game work; it isn't used to identify or track you, so there's no cookie banner to click through.

Who your browser talks to

  • This site (hosted on GitHub Pages), to load the game. Like any web host, GitHub may log requests, including your IP address.
  • Coinbase (api.exchange.coinbase.com and ws-feed.exchange.coinbase.com), for live prices and price history. These are public market data feeds: your browser sends nothing about you or your game, but Coinbase sees your IP address, as any server you connect to does. Coinbase's servers sit behind Cloudflare, which tries to set a short-lived security cookie (__cf_bm, for telling people from bots) on Coinbase's own domain. The game refuses it for price history; for the live price connection, browsers decide (Safari and Firefox block it by default). This site can't read it. See Coinbase's privacy policy.

Nothing else: no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. The site's Content Security Policy blocks everything except these two.
